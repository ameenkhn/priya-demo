Child & Pediatric Care

Pediatric & Child Health Questions

When should I bring my baby for the first pediatric visit?
Your baby’s first visit is usually recommended within 3–7 days after birth, or earlier if advised. Regular check-ups help monitor feeding, weight gain, jaundice, growth, and overall newborn health.
What vaccinations does my child need?
Vaccinations protect children from serious illnesses. We follow the Indian Academy of Pediatrics (IAP) immunization schedule and provide personalized vaccine recommendations based on your child’s age and health needs.
What if my child has missed one or more vaccinations?
Missing a vaccine dose is common, and in most cases your child can safely continue with a catch-up vaccination schedule without restarting the entire series.
  • Bring your child’s vaccination card for review.
  • We will create a personalized catch-up plan.
  • Timely vaccination helps protect against preventable diseases.
When should I worry about my child’s growth and development?
Every child develops at their own pace. However, concerns such as poor weight gain, delayed milestones, speech delays, or behavioral concerns should be evaluated. Regular well-child visits help ensure your child is growing and developing appropriately.
Do you provide breastfeeding and nutrition support?
Yes. We offer lactation counselling for breastfeeding challenges and expert nutrition guidance for infants, children, and adolescents, including weaning, picky eating, growth concerns, and healthy eating habits.
Do you treat common childhood illnesses?
Yes. We evaluate and manage common pediatric conditions such as fever, cough, cold, diarrhea, vomiting, allergies, asthma, skin problems, and other routine childhood illnesses. We also guide parents on warning signs that need urgent medical attention.
Do you provide developmental assessments for children?
Yes. We monitor your child’s growth, developmental milestones, speech and language skills, social interaction, learning abilities, and behavior. Early identification of concerns allows timely intervention and better outcomes.
Do you offer adolescent health consultations?
Yes. We provide comprehensive adolescent care, including growth and puberty concerns, nutrition counselling, menstrual health, lifestyle guidance, emotional well-being, and preventive healthcare support.
Neuro-Spine Care

Neuro-Spine & Surgery Questions

When should I consult a Neuro-Spine Surgeon?
You should consider a consultation if you have persistent neck pain, back pain, numbness, tingling, weakness in the arms or legs, difficulty walking, loss of balance, or symptoms that do not improve with medication and physiotherapy.
Does every spine problem require surgery?
No. Most neck and back pain conditions can be managed with medications, lifestyle modifications, physiotherapy, and pain management techniques. Surgery is recommended only when necessary, such as in cases of nerve compression, spinal instability, fractures, tumors, or when conservative treatment fails.
What are the warning signs of a serious spine problem?
Seek medical attention if you experience:
  • Progressive weakness in the arms or legs
  • Difficulty walking or maintaining balance
  • Loss of bladder or bowel control
  • Severe pain following an injury or fall
  • Persistent numbness or tingling
Early diagnosis can help prevent permanent nerve damage.
Can surgery help with slipped discs, cervical spondylosis, and lumbar spondylosis?
Yes. When symptoms such as severe pain, weakness, numbness, or nerve compression do not respond to non-surgical treatment, modern spine surgery can provide significant relief and improve quality of life. Your treatment plan will be tailored to your specific condition.
What conditions are treated at your Neuro-Spine Clinic?
We provide expert evaluation and treatment for:
  • Neck pain and back pain
  • Cervical and lumbar spondylosis
  • Disc prolapse (slipped disc)
  • Brain and spine tumors
  • Head and spinal injuries
  • Nerve injuries
  • Spinal fractures
  • Stroke and epilepsy requiring surgical management
  • Chronic pain conditions
